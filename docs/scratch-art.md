# Scratch card art

Ten Drawing cards combine original scenery with character and prop vectors from
[Google’s Noto Emoji collection](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/tree/99701e07d3d1fe1bdbcdb14964547f0fd592966f/2D/svg).
The original dinosaur, shark, and night sky cards are drawn in code.

The source SVGs are stored unchanged under `src/discovery/art/`, with descriptive
filenames. They are Apache 2.0 artwork from Google LLC. The
[license and credit](../public/scratch-art-license.txt) are also shipped in the
web and desktop builds. The app creates each picture locally at startup.
